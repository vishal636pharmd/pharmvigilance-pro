Add Bill by Text

Open your Apollo invoice page → copy all text → paste below. Works for Apollo, MedPlus, and any pharmacy.

1
Open your Apollo SMS bill link Tap the link from Apollo SMS → enter OTP → invoice page opens
2
Select all text on the invoice page On phone: long press on page → tap Select All → Copy
On PC: press Ctrl+A on the invoice page → then Ctrl+C
3
Paste below and tap Extract Tap the text box below → long press → Paste → tap Extract
