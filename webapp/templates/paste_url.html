Process Your Apollo Bill

Paste your Apollo invoice URL — your browser fetches it directly using your active session, so OTP authentication works perfectly.

1
Open your Apollo SMS bill link Tap the link Apollo sent you via SMS. It starts with apmails.in or invoice.apollopharmacy.in
2
Enter the OTP on Apollo's page Apollo sends OTP to your registered number. Enter it — the invoice page opens showing your medicines.
3
Copy the URL and paste below Copy the full URL from your browser address bar (starts with invoice.apollopharmacy.in/afterotpvalidation...) → paste below → tap Extract.
How this works:
Your browser fetches the Apollo page directly using your active OTP session — the same way you'd open any link. PVPro never sees your OTP or phone number. The bill data is extracted and saved to your profile only.
